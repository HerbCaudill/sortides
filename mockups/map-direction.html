Sortides – map direction

Direction B refined: a sidebar with a base toggle (Barcelona or Tamariu) and a mode toggle (date nights or day trips). The tabs show all four combinations and the phone layout, using the same fictional week of 26 October 2026.

Why: Date nights in Barcelona zoom the map to the city and switch it to a night palette, so the toggle changes the scale and the mood as well as the list. The list is grouped by when, because for a night out the date matters most.

Tradeoff: The bars in the Gòtic and Raval cluster tightly, so labels need care at this zoom. The dark map is a proposal and easy to drop.

Why: Day trips zoom out to the region with drive-time rings. City exhibitions and places collapse into one “In the city” cluster, and the list is grouped by how far you have to go.

Tradeoff: Exhibitions in town count as day outings here, which matches your split but puts a two-hour museum visit next to a full day in Cardona.

Why: From Tamariu the map centers on the Baix Empordà, where nights out mean Palafrugell, the coast and the occasional trip to Girona for a VOSE film.

Tradeoff: There are far fewer events up here, so this list will often be short. That’s honest, but it may look thin compared to Barcelona.

Why: Rings around the house show what’s 30 minutes or an hour away. Places farther than the map shows get an edge marker (Besalú), and an item can link to a related one at the other base.

Tradeoff: Places between the two bases, like Girona or Montserrat, need a rule for which base they belong to, or they’ll show up under both.

Why: On a phone the map fills the screen, the two toggles float at the top, and the sidebar becomes a bottom sheet that can be raised for the list or lowered to explore the map. Tapping an item turns the sheet into its detail and centers the pin. There are no hotkeys on the phone.

Tradeoff: With the sheet halfway up, only the top part of the map is visible, so the map needs to re-center whenever you switch base or mode.
